# 1. Introduction

## 1.1 What is OLA?

**OLA is an AI assistant that takes your cross-border B2B sales from first inquiry to closed deal — and beyond to renewal.**

![OLA Feature Overview](/img/en/1.1.png)

1. **Listen**: Understands what you and your customers say on calls, in messages, and in emails — supporting Mandarin, Cantonese, English, and any mix
2. **Remember**: Automatically organizes every interaction into each customer's continuous profile — who they are, what they need, what they care about, how they make decisions
3. **Act**: Drafts quotes, responds to inquiries, follows up, reminds about renewals — and every price comes from your real product catalog, never invented
4. **Coach**: Gives you real-time guidance while you're on calls or writing emails — "the customer just raised this concern, here's how to respond"

## 1.3 OLA's Three-Layer Architecture

OLA is built on three interconnected layers:

![OLA Three-Layer Architecture](/img/en/1.3.png)

```
┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  L1 · Data Capture (data flows in automatically)    │
│  Call recordings  WhatsApp  Email  Web forms  Visits │
└──────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
                         ↓
┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  L2 · Customer Profile (a continuously updated brain)│
│  Needs  Objections  Price sensitivity  Decision style│
│  Funnel stage  Churn risk                            │
└──────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
                         ↓
┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  L3 · Execution Automation                           │
│  Inquiry response  Quote generation  Real-time coach │
│  Renewal outreach                                    │
└──────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

- **L1 · Data**: Automatically captures calls, emails, and WhatsApp messages — no change to your workflow required.
- **L2 · Profile**: Information is synthesized into each customer's continuous profile, owned by the company, not lost when a rep leaves.
- **L3 · Execution**: Based on each profile, OLA acts at every funnel stage — responding to inquiries, generating quotes, following up.

**AskOLA is your unified entry point** to all three layers.
