# 3. Call Recordings & Analysis

So far we've covered you actively asking OLA for things. But OLA's real power is that **data flows in on its own**.

The L1 data capture layer is OLA's eyes and ears. This chapter covers call recording upload and analysis.

---

## 3.1 What This Feature Does

Upload a call recording and the system will:

- **Auto-transcribe** the entire recording — never manually log a call again
- **Extract key information**: customer product needs, objections, budget signals, delivery requirements
- **Analyze what went well and what could improve** — like having a senior sales coach review your call
- **Provide specific improvement suggestions** based on your company's successful deal patterns

---

## 3.2 Before You Upload

Confirm the following before uploading:

1. **File format**: `.mp3`, `.m4a`, `.wav`, `.aac` are all supported
2. **File size**: Maximum 500MB per file
3. **Duration**: Minimum 1 minute; recordings over 25 minutes are automatically chunked
4. **Language**: Mandarin, English, Cantonese, and mixed are all supported

---

## 3.3 How to Upload

### Option 1: Via the File Module

1. Click **File** in the left navigation
2. Click **+ Upload File**, select your recording
3. Transcription starts automatically after upload (typically 3–5 minutes)
4. Click the file name to view results in the right-side drawer

### Option 2: Directly in AskOLA

Click the attachment icon next to the AskOLA input box, upload the recording, then continue the conversation:
> Summarize the key points from this call

---

## 3.4 What's in the Analysis Report

### 1. Call Summary

> This call lasted 12 minutes 34 seconds. The customer primarily asked about delivery timeline and minimum order quantity for the wireless temperature sensor. They accepted the price but asked if delivery could be shortened from 30 to 20 days. The call ended with the customer saying they needed to confirm internally before responding.

### 2. Extracted Customer Intelligence

| Category | What the Customer Said |
|----------|----------------------|
| Product needed | Wireless Temperature Sensor |
| Quantity | 300 units initial order |
| Price acceptance | Accepted current pricing |
| Main concern | Can delivery be under 20 days? |
| Next step | Customer will confirm internally within 3 days |

### 3. Call Highlights

> ✅ You proactively compared against competitors when introducing the product — this was helpful for the customer's decision-making.

### 4. Improvement Suggestions

> ⚠️ At 4:12, the customer asked "can delivery be any faster?" and you responded "let me check." Consider giving a preliminary answer next time (e.g., "fastest we can do is 25 days, I'll confirm if 20 is possible") so the customer feels you're actively driving it forward.

---

## 3.5 Important Notes

- Recordings are for internal use only and are never shared with third parties
- **We recommend obtaining customer consent before recording calls** — check local laws
- Poor signal quality or heavy background noise may reduce transcription accuracy
