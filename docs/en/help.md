# 5. FAQ & Troubleshooting

## 5.1 General Questions

### AskOLA didn't understand my question. What do I do?

Try rephrasing. If you're searching for a customer, use their full company name. For products, try the SKU instead of the product name. If it still doesn't work, contact support.

> 💡 Tip: If the same question fails 3 times with longer phrasing, switching to a shorter, more specific keyword is often more effective than writing a longer sentence.

### I made a change by mistake. Can I undo it?

Changes cannot be undone with one click. However, every modification is logged — contact your admin to view the change history and revert it manually. **This is why the confirmation prompt before every change matters — always read it carefully.**

### Will AskOLA leak my company's data?

No. All customer, product, and quote data is stored only in your company's own database. All transmissions are encrypted, and your customer conversations are never used to train AI models.

### I don't have permission for a certain action. What now?

Contact your company's system administrator to grant the appropriate role permissions to your account.

### Can I send a quote directly to a customer from OLA?

Yes. Once the quote is finalized, you can send it directly to the customer's email via the system, or download it as a file and send it yourself.

## 5.2 Errors & Troubleshooting

### Recording upload failed

| Likely Cause | Solution |
|-------------|----------|
| Unsupported file format | Must be .mp3, .m4a, .wav, or .aac |
| File exceeds 500MB | Compress the file and retry, or contact admin |
| Network unstable | Switch networks and try again |

### Quote generation shows "product has no price"

The product in your catalog has no unit price set. Add the price in the Merchandise catalog first, then regenerate the quote. This is OLA's zero-hallucination principle — the system never invents prices.

### System is slow to respond

Likely causes: slow network connection, or high concurrent usage. If it's unresponsive for more than 30 seconds, contact technical support.
