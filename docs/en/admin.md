## 4.2 Ask AskOLA as a Manager

Managers can ask AskOLA directly — no dashboards to navigate:

> What's our revenue this month compared to last month?
>
> Which sales rep is performing best? What are they doing right?
>
> How are our top 5 customers doing? Any risks?
>
> Which customers have had zero interaction in the past 3 months?

## 4.3 Permission Management

Admins can manage team permissions in **Settings → Members**:

| Role | Permissions |
|------|-------------|
| Admin | Full access including delete, export, view all sales data |
| Member | View and edit their own customers, no delete |

To grant or change a rep's permissions, an admin makes the change here.
