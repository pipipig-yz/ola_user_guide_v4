# 2.2 Modifying Records

This section shows you how to **directly update** existing records in the system via AskOLA — no need to hunt through form pages field by field.

> ⚠️ **Important**: Before every modification, the system will show you exactly what's changing and ask for your confirmation. Changes only take effect after you click "Yes". All modifications are logged with the operator, timestamp, and what changed.

---

## 2.2.1 Update Customer Information

**When to use:**
- A customer changed their contact person or phone number
- You want to update a customer's follow-up status (e.g., "In Negotiation" → "Closed Won")
- You found an error in previously entered data

**Step-by-step example:**

**Step 1:** Type in AskOLA:
> Change XX Company's phone number to 138XXXXXXXX

**Step 2:** The system finds the record, shows the current info, and asks for confirmation:
> Found customer "XX Company". Current phone: 136XXXXXXXX
> You want to change it to: 138XXXXXXXX
> Confirm? [Yes] [No]

**Step 3:** Click "Yes" — the change takes effect immediately.

## 2.2.2 Update Product Information

**When to use:**
- Raw material costs increased, need to update selling price
- Received new inventory, need to update stock count
- A product is discontinued, need to deactivate it

**Example:**

> Change the wireless temperature sensor unit price from $68 to $75

> ⚠️ Note: Changing a product's price does **not** automatically update already-sent quotes. If a quote also needs updating, edit it separately.

**Screenshot:**

![Update merchandise price](/img/en/2.2.2_change_merchandise_price.png)

---

## 2.2.3 Update a Quote

**When to use:**
- Customer requests a quantity change or product swap
- Need to apply a discount
- Delivery terms or payment conditions changed
- Quote is about to expire and needs an extension

**Examples:**

**Change quantity:**
> Change the wireless temperature sensor quantity in XX Company's latest quote from 200 to 350 units

**Apply discount:**
> Apply a 10% discount to XX Company's latest quote

**Extend expiry:**
> Extend quote 2025-0312's expiry date to June 30
