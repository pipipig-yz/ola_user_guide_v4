# 2.1 Querying Information

This section shows you how to use AskOLA to **quickly retrieve** customer data, product information, quotes, customer profiles, and call records.

---

## 2.1.1 Query Customer Information

Quickly look up a customer's **contact details**, **follow-up history**, or **past orders** — or filter your customer list by region, industry, or time.

**Query templates:**

> Look up the contact info for XX Company
>
> List all customers in Guangdong
>
> Which customers haven't been followed up with in over a month?
>
> Which customers have historical orders over $500K?

**Result example:**

![Customer query result](/img/en/2.1.1_search_user.png)

---

## 2.1.2 Query Product Information

Quickly look up a product's **price**, **stock**, or **specifications** — or filter your catalog by category or price range.

**Query templates:**

> What's the price and stock of the wireless temperature sensor?
>
> Look up the details for SKU WTS-2024
>
> Do we have any products priced between $200 and $300?
>
> Which products have fewer than 100 units in stock?

**Result example:**

![Product query result](/img/en/2.1.2_search_product.png)

---

## 2.1.3 Query Quotes

Look up a quote's **total amount**, **current status**, and **negotiation progress** — or filter quotes by time period or status.

**Query templates:**

> Show me the most recent quote sent to XX Company
>
> What's the current status of quote number 2025-0312?
>
> Which quotes from last month are still pending customer confirmation?
>
> How many quotes include the "Wireless Temperature Sensor"?
