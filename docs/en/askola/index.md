# 2. AskOLA AI Assistant

AskOLA is your primary interface for everything in OLA. Whether you need to find information, update records, or create new ones — just say it. No menus to learn, no forms to fill.

This chapter has three sections:

- **[2.1 Querying Information](/en/askola/search-en)**: Look up customers, products, quotes
- **[2.2 Modifying Records](/en/askola/edit-en)**: Update info, prices, quotes
- **[2.3 Creating New Records](/en/askola/create-en)**: Add customers, products, quotes

---

## Opening AskOLA

After logging in, click **AskOLA** in the left navigation bar to open the chat interface.

You can open it from any page in the system without interrupting what you're doing.
