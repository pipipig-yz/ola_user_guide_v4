2.3 Creating New Records

This section shows you how to use AskOLA to create customer profiles, add products, and generate quotes — no forms required, just describe what you need.

2.3.1 Create a Customer Profile​

When to use:

  • You collected business cards at a trade show and need to batch-add customers
  • A new customer sent an inquiry and you need to log them first

Required information:

  • Customer company name (required)
  • Contact person's name (required)
  • Phone number or email — at least one (required)

Options:

Option 1 — Tell it all at once:

Add a new customer: XX Trading Co., contact Manager Li, phone 138XXXXXXXX, electronics wholesale, based in Shanghai

Option 2 — Let the system guide you:

Create a new customer profile

Option 3 — Extract from an email (recommended):

Copy-paste the customer's introduction email directly into the chat:

Extract the customer info from this message and create a profile: [paste email content]

2.3.2 Add a Product​

When to use:

  • A new product needs to be listed
  • You received new pricing from a supplier and need to enter it

Required information:

  • Product name (required)
  • SKU / serial number (required)
  • Unit price (required)

Options:

Add a single product:

Add a new product: Wireless Temperature Sensor, SKU WTS-2024, unit price $68, stock 500 units, category Industrial Sensors

Batch add multiple products:

Add all of these products to the catalog: [paste product list]

Screenshot:

2.3.3 Create a Quote​

When to use:

  • A customer sent an inquiry and you need to turn it into a quote quickly
  • You want to copy a previous quote and adjust quantities

Required information:

  • Customer
  • Products and quantities

Options:

Tell it all at once (recommended):

Create a quote for XX Company:

  • Wireless Temperature Sensor × 200 units
  • Industrial Pressure Sensor × 100 units Payment: wire transfer, validity: 30 days

Copy a previous quote:

Copy the last quote we sent to XX Company, keep the products, double all quantities

:::info Zero-Hallucination Quoting When AskOLA generates a quote, every price comes from your product catalog. The system never estimates or guesses prices.

If a product has no price set, the system will explicitly tell you to add a price first before generating the quote. :::
